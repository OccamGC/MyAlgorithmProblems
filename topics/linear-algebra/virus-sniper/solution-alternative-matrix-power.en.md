# Virus Sniper — Full-exponent matrix powering

[The C++17 implementation](alternative-matrix-power.cpp) by Codex (GPT-6) processes every bit of the decimal time after base conversion.

Raise the transition matrix to $T$ directly. Reducing a matrix exponent modulo $P-1$ can give a wrong result. At most two dense matrix multiplications per bit give $O(bd^3)$ matrix time and $O(d^2+b)$ workspace for dimension $d$ and $b$ bits. This is a direct check for small matrices and large exponents.
